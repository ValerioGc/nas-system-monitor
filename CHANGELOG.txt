# Changelog

All notable changes to nas-system-monitor are documented in this file.

## [0.9.0]

### Changed

- The Rust tests moved from `backend/tests/` into the package they test (`backend/axum/tests/`),
  which has `autotests = false`: rust-analyzer loads only the files of the package and reported the
  test modules as unresolved (E0583), although they built and passed.
- Development dependencies updated as Dependabot proposed (Vite 8.3.4, Vitest 5.0.4, Playwright 1.64,
  ESLint 10.12, Prettier 3.9.10, postcss-combine-duplicated-selectors 11 and the others), TypeScript
  kept at 6.0.3; in the pipelines actions/setup-node 7.1.0, upload-artifact 7.0.2 and
  download-artifact 8.0.2; serde_json 1.0.154, tokio 1.53.3 and hyper 1.12.0.

## [0.1.16]

### Security

- The socket of the app answers the launcher only (root, the owner of the folder of the sockets, or
  the app itself): another app of the NAS can no longer reach its API with headers it made up
  (`launcher_only.rs`).

## [0.1.9]

### Changed

- A sensor in the widget shows a filled pin on the soft accent, apart from the outline of the
  others at a glance.
- The texts of the interface, in Italian and English, are impersonal and shorter: no ellipsis,
  colon or semicolon, no names of internal programs, commands or variables.
- The filter of the idle processes is a button beside the other filters, "Hide idle", with what it
  hides in its tooltip. Only the body of the process table scrolls, under a heading that stays.
- In the settings the RAM unit and the refresh sit on the line of their label, and the title has
  no line under it.
- Headings, fields, page header and hidden labels come from `_nas-controls.scss` of the shared
  library instead of a copy of the same rules.

### Removed

- Styles never applied: `.page_panel` and the primary variant of `.action_button`.

## [0.1.8]

### Added

- The page of the processes can hide those that wait and use nothing (no processor, graphics or
  memory: the threads of the kernel, mostly), with a switch beside the filter.

### Changed

- The files of `assets/`, with a hash of their content in the name, are served with
  `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`, the page with `no-cache`: the browser keeps
  the scripts and the styles between visits instead of asking for them again through the gateway
  and the launcher.
- Readings close together share one look at the machine: within a second of the last, the page,
  the widget of the launcher and other browsers get the same one, and the share of the CPU is
  measured over a real interval.
- The launcher bridge (under `askNasShell`), the shared styles, icons, the loading state, switches,
  number steppers, the native dialog under `CommonModal`, the stored documents and the build,
  package and release scripts come from the shared library `@valeriogc/nas-base-app`. A number
  typed out of its range now shows again the value kept, which never changed.

## [0.1.7]

### Changed

- The table of the documents is `STRICT`. An existing database does not open: install from
  scratch.
- The settings are kept by the backend, in a SQLite database of the application
  (`api/documents/preferences`), never the launcher's: the app, the widget and every browser that
  opens the NAS share them. What this browser kept before is carried over once.
- In the frame of the launcher the application keeps the rules of its old sandbox by itself
  (`src/services/frameGuard.ts`, called first in `main.ts`): no window of its own, no
  `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt` dialog, no navigation of the page that holds it. The launcher dropped
  the sandbox, which on its origin was no wall and made the browser warn: nothing else changes.

### Added

- The sensors of the widget can be moved up and down from the settings: the widget shows them
  in that order.

## [0.1.0]

### Added

- The monitor of the NAS: temperature, use and power of processor, graphics, memory and disks,
  each kind in its own section, with the sensors to show in the widget, six at most.
- The processes of the NAS, filtered by user, NAS and system, with a context menu that terminates
  a process or restarts its service; an essential one may only be restarted.
- The widget of the launcher, drawn by this application: chosen sensors, a monitor that folds away
  and the list of processes chosen in the options.

### Changed

- The surfaces follow the theme chosen in the launcher for its type, through the colours of the
  context; outside the launcher the application keeps its own.
